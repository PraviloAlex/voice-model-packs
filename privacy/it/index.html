Kukuluku Voice works without the internet. Speech is recognized and translated right on your computer.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking
01

Does the app hear me all the time?

No. Only while you dictate - by default, while you hold Ctrl+Win.More ›Less

The microphone also turns on with F9 (insert by voice) and when you test it. A model on your computer recognizes the sound; the temporary audio file is deleted right away. Recordings are kept only if you turned on «Save audio recordings» (off by default).

02

Where do my words go?

Into the window where your cursor is, and into History on your disk.More ›Less

Usually the text goes in through the clipboard, and after 2 seconds the previous text comes back. If Windows clipboard history (Win+V) or clipboard sync is on, Windows may keep a dictation and send it to Microsoft's cloud. To turn History off or clear it: «Settings → Privacy».

03

Does translation go online?

No. A model on your computer translates.More ›Less

To take selected text, the app copies it through the clipboard for a moment and puts the clipboard back as it was. Language pairs download once.

04

What does the app know about other apps?

Only the name of the app it types into. The text field - only if you turned on «Notice my corrections».More ›Less

By the name it picks how to insert. «Notice my corrections» is off until you turn it on: then, after a dictation, the app reads the field it typed into to learn your corrections. It doesn't read window titles, password fields or other apps' files.

05

What is on my disk?

History, Dictionary, text snippets, settings and audio recordings if you turned them on - plain files in your Windows profile.More ›Less

History is on from the start; you can turn it off. There are also work logs - they never contain what you dictated. All of it stays on your computer.

06

When does the app go online?

Only to download a speech or translation model.More ›Less

Models live on GitHub, and the app checks that every file arrived whole and unaltered. GitHub sees your computer's address and the file name, as with any download; nothing reaches us. If GitHub is unreachable, a Whisper model may download from the backup site Hugging Face - there the app doesn't check the file.

07

What if the app breaks?

The app itself sends nothing: an email goes only if you send it.More ›Less

«Report a problem» packs the logs into an archive and opens an email - you attach the archive yourself. Emails are kept for up to 12 months; we read them only to answer you and fix the app. By sending an email, you agree that it is kept in Gmail (USA).

08

How do I delete everything?

Uninstall the app - Windows removes it with History, Dictionary and models (except a models folder you chose yourself).More ›Less

«Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Kukuluku Voice → Uninstall». Delete your own models folder by hand. To clear only History: the app's «Settings → Privacy → Clear history».

09

Personal data collection

We collect none from anyone, children included: only an email reaches us, if you write one yourself.

10

Who is responsible, and what can I do?

The Kukuluku developer, Argentina, kukuluku.app@gmail.com.More ›Less

This policy covers the Microsoft Store version. Write to us to find out, correct or delete what we hold about you (in practice, only your emails) - we answer within 10 days. You can complain to your country's data protection authority.

11

What leaves your computer

  • A model file request → GitHub, when a speech or translation model downloads
  • Backup for a Whisper model → Hugging Face, only if GitHub is unreachable
  • App updates → Microsoft Store, like any Store app
  • A «Report a problem» email → Gmail (Google, USA), only if you send it yourself
  • Everything else - nothing.
